Sutherland Shire Tiling

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide tiling services throughout the Sutherland Shire from Cronulla down to the Royal National Park areas. The team have been working in the Shire for alot of years now and we know the area well. We do bathroom renovations, outdoor tiling, pool surrounds, floor tiling and waterproofing for residential properties.

Living in the Shire means alot of outdoor entertaining. As such we do plenty of outdoor tiling work including pool surrounds, alfresco areas, patios and balconies. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling understand the requirements for outdoor tiling in a coastal environment where tiles need to handle weather and salt air.

Pool Surrounds and Outdoor Areas

Pool surrounds are one of our specialties in the Sutherland Shire. The team have tiled hundreds of pool areas over the years from small plunge pools through to full size swimming pools. We use the correct adhesives for wet areas and make sure the falls are right for drainage. Non slip tiles are essential around pools and we can advise on the best options.

For outdoor entertaining areas we work with porcelain pavers, natural stone, travertine and other outdoor tiles. The team understand what works in coastal conditions and what doesnt. Not all tiles are suitable for outdoor use and we can help you make the right choice.

Bathroom Renovations

Bathroom renovations are another big part of what we do in the Shire. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling handle everything from the demolition through to final tile installation. We are licensed waterproofers and can provide certificates on completion which is required for any wet area work.

Whether its a coastal style bathroom with subway tiles or something more modern with large format tiles we can help. The team have done alot of bathroom work in the Sutherland Shire over the years and understand what works in the local area.
