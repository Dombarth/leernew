Bathroom Tiling Sydney

Specialist bathroom and shower tiling services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the areas of bathroom tiling for residential properties across Sydney. Bathrooms are where alot of our work happens. Shower recesses, bathroom floors, feature walls, vanity splashbacks. The team have been tiling bathrooms for over eighteen years and we understand what it takes to get them right.

Shower Tiling

Showers are the most critical part of bathroom tiling. Everything needs to be right. The waterproofing needs to be perfect, the falls need to drain water properly, the tiles need precise cuts around fixtures and niches. Had a job come in from a customer in Coogee where their existing shower had water pooling in the corner instead of draining. The previous tiler hadnt set the falls correctly. We retiled the shower floor with proper falls to the drain and the problem was solved.

For shower floors we use small format tiles like mosaics because they conform better to the falls. Large tiles on shower floors can cause water to pool. The grout lines also provide some grip which is important in a wet area. We recommend epoxy grout in showers because it doesnt absorb water and resists mould.

Shower Niches

Shower niches have become standard in most bathroom renovations. They look good and provide useful storage. But they need to be done properly. The niche needs to be waterproofed correctly with the membrane continuing into the niche. The floor of the niche needs a slight fall so water drains out rather than pooling inside.

We install niches in various sizes and can accommodate multiple niches if thats what you want. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have done hundreds of shower niche installations and we know how to get them watertight.

Bathroom Floors

Bathroom floors need tiles with appropriate slip ratings. In Australia theres standards for wet area floor tiles and we make sure the tiles used meet those requirements. The substrate needs to be level and the falls need to direct water toward the floor waste.

Large format tiles are popular for bathroom floors now. Less grout lines means easier cleaning and a more modern look. We do alot of 600x600mm floor tiles in bathrooms and some even bigger. The key is proper substrate preparation so the tiles sit flat without lippage.

Feature Walls

Feature walls in bathrooms add visual interest and can make a small space feel more designed. We've installed feature walls using mosaics, textured tiles, natural stone and coloured tiles. The feature wall might be behind the vanity, on the back wall of the shower, or a full wall in a larger bathroom.

Had a customer in Rose Bay who wanted a marble feature wall behind her freestanding bath. We sourced bookmatched marble tiles so the veining pattern flowed across the wall. Took some planning to get the layout right but the result was worth it.

Bathroom Wall Tiling

General wall tiling in bathrooms needs to consider waterproofing requirements. In wet areas like showers the waterproofing membrane goes under the tiles. In other areas of the bathroom the walls still need proper preparation and the right adhesive for the conditions.

We install all types of bathroom wall tiles. Ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, glass tiles, mosaics. Subway tiles remain popular for bathroom walls and we do alot of those. Large format rectified tiles give a cleaner modern look with minimal grout lines.

Vanity Splashbacks

The area behind the vanity gets splashed regularly so it makes sense to tile it. We tile vanity splashbacks to match the rest of the bathroom or as a contrasting feature. The tiling needs to work around mirrors and any wall-mounted tapware if thats what youve got.

Tile Types for Bathrooms

Porcelain tiles are the most common choice for bathrooms. Theyre durable, water resistant and come in endless styles and colours. Ceramic tiles work well too especially for walls. Natural stone like marble and travertine looks beautiful but needs sealing and more maintenance. Glass tiles and mosaics are used for feature areas.

Timber look tiles are popular for bathroom floors now. They give the warmth of timber without the water damage concerns. The team can advise on what tiles suit your bathroom if your not sure.
