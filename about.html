About Sydney Premium Tiling

Eighteen years of quality tiling and waterproofing

Who We Are

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the aspects of wall and floor tiling, bathroom renovations, waterproofing and outdoor tiling for both residential and commercial properties. The team have been servicing Sydney for over eighteen years and we are fully licensed to carry out all types of tiling work.

We handle everything from small bathroom repairs through to full commercial fitouts. As such the tilers at Sydney Premium Tiling are equipped to take on jobs that other tilers may not have the experiance for. All our work is guaranteed and we are fully insured for public liability and workers compensation.

Our Experience

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling pride themselves on quality workmanship and attention to detail. Having completed over three thousand projects across Sydney we understand what works and what doesnt. From heritage bathroom restorations in Paddington to modern apartment renovations in Chatswood, we've done alot of it over the years.

We dont send subcontractors to your job. When you book Sydney Premium Tiling you get our own experienced tilers who have been with us for years. Each tiler on the team have their speciality areas - some are better with natural stone, others with large format tiles or intricate mosaic work.

What Makes Us Different

We started small and have stayed small on purpose. Its not about doing hundreds of jobs a month for us. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling focus on doing each job properly. We still get most of our work from word of mouth recommendations which tells you something.

For bathroom renovations we handle the complete project including waterproofing, screeding, tiling and grouting. We work with your plumber and electrician to coordinate the work properly. If you dont have trades lined up we can recommend reliable people we work with regularly.

Our Approach

Every job starts with a proper assessment. We come out to look at the site before quoting because you cant price a job properly from photos. The substrate preparation is critical - if the surface isnt right the tiles wont last. Thats why we spend time getting the prep work perfect even if it takes an extra day.

We use quality adhesives and grouts from Mapei and Ardex. For waterproofing we prefer using sheet membranes in wet areas rather than just liquid membrane. It costs more but its worth it for the peace of mind. All our waterproofing work comes with certificates for your records.

Areas We Service

We service customers across Sydney including the eastern suburbs, north shore, inner west, northern beaches and Hills District areas. For larger commercial projects we also work in Parramatta, Sydney CBD and surrounding regions. Most weeks we're working everywhere from Bondi to Baulkham Hills depending on what jobs are booked.

Check our Google reviews to see what previous customers say. Most mention the same things - professional, punctual, clean worksite, competitive pricing. We think thats pretty accurate.
