Floor Tiling Sydney

Quality floor tile installations for homes and businesses

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the areas of floor tiling for residential and commercial properties throughout Sydney. The team have laid floors in everything from small bathroom renovations through to large commercial spaces and weve been doing it for over eighteen years now.

Residential Floor Tiling

Most of our floor tiling work is in homes. Living rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, laundries, hallways. Had a job come in from a family in Randwick who wanted their entire ground floor retiled. The existing tiles were those terracotta pavers from the eighties and they wanted something more modern. We removed about 80 square metres of old tiles, levelled the substrate where needed, and installed 600x600 porcelain tiles throughout. Took about five days and the difference was night and day.

Floor preparation is where alot of the work happens with floor tiling. If the substrate isnt level the tiles will lippage and look wrong. If its not solid enough the tiles will crack. As such we spend the time on screeding and levelling before any tiles go down. Its the foundation of good floor tiling work.

Large Format Floor Tiles

Large format tiles have become really popular for floors. 600x600mm is standard now but we also do 600x1200mm and even bigger. The bigger tiles mean less grout lines which gives a cleaner modern look. They also make rooms feel larger.

Large format tiles need proper substrate preparation and the right adhesive. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling are experienced with large format installations and we know how to avoid lippage and ensure proper coverage. We've done alot of large format floor work across Sydney.

Natural Stone Floors

Natural stone floors like marble and travertine look beautiful but they require different handling than porcelain or ceramic. Stone is porous so it needs sealing. It also requires specific adhesives and more careful handling during installation. We did a marble floor in a house in Vaucluse and it looked absolutely stunning when it was finished. Natural stone does cost more but the result speaks for itself.

For natural stone we use flexible adhesives and apply sealers after grouting. The sealing protects the stone from staining and makes it easier to clean. We can advise on the right stone for your application because not all stone suits all areas.

Timber Look Tiles

Timber look tiles are another option we see alot now. They give you the warmth of timber floors without the maintenance issues. No water damage worries in bathrooms or laundries. No scratches from pets. The technology has come a long way and some of these tiles look very realistic.

Timber look tiles are usually rectangular so they get laid in a staggered pattern like real floorboards. We ensure the pattern looks natural with random staggers rather than a repetitive pattern. Its one of those things that makes a difference to the final result.

Commercial Floor Tiling

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have completed floor tiling projects for retail shops, restaurants, cafes, office buildings and medical centres. Commercial floors need to handle heavy foot traffic so tile selection matters. We work with builders and project managers on commercial fitouts and understand the importance of meeting deadlines.

For commercial projects we can work after hours or on weekends to minimise disruption to your business. We've done plenty of retail fitouts where the shop needed to be open during the day so we worked nights to get the job done.

Floor Preparation and Screeding

The substrate is everything with floor tiling. We assess every floor before quoting to understand what preparation will be needed. Some floors just need cleaning and priming. Others need full screeding to level them out. Old adhesive residue needs to be removed. Cracks need to be addressed.

We use self levelling compounds for minor levelling and sand cement screeds for more significant falls or level changes. Getting this right is critical because floor tiles show every imperfection in the substrate. The team know what each floor needs.

Underfloor Heating

We also install tiles over underfloor heating systems. The cables or mats need to be properly embedded and the right adhesives used to cope with the thermal expansion. If you're thinking about underfloor heating we can discuss how it works with tiled floors. We've done quite a few of these jobs in bathrooms and living areas.
