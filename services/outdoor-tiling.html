Outdoor Tiling Sydney

Pool surrounds, balconies, patios and alfresco areas

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the areas of outdoor tiling for residential and commercial properties across Sydney. Outdoor tiling has different requirements than indoor work. The tiles need to handle weather extremes, the adhesives need to cope with movement, and proper drainage is essential. The team have been doing outdoor tiling for over eighteen years and we understand what works in Sydney conditions.

Pool Surrounds

Pool tiling is one of our specialty areas. Pool surrounds need non slip tiles that are comfortable to walk on barefoot but wont get slippery when wet. We use tiles with appropriate slip ratings and textured finishes. Coping tiles around pool edges need to be installed properly so theyre safe and look good.

Had a pool surround job come in from a customer in Cremorne. They wanted to replace the old exposed aggregate with porcelain pavers. We removed the existing surface, checked the substrate, and installed large format porcelain tiles with proper falls to drainage points. The pool area looks completely different now and the owners are happy with how it turned out.

Balcony Tiling

Balcony tiling is critical work. If the waterproofing fails water gets into the structure below and causes serious damage. We're licenced waterproofers so we can handle both the waterproofing and tiling on balcony projects. We provide waterproofing certificates for all balcony work.

The falls on a balcony need to direct water toward drainage points so it doesnt pool on the surface. Typically we create a fall of around 1:100 minimum toward floor wastes or drainage channels. The tiles then follow that fall. Getting this right prevents water sitting on your balcony and potential leaks into the building.

We've seen alot of balconies over the years where the waterproofing or drainage wasnt done properly. Water damage to the structure underneath is expensive to fix. As such we make sure balcony jobs are done correctly the first time.

Patios and Alfresco Areas

Patio tiling transforms outdoor entertaining areas. Whether its a covered alfresco attached to the house or an open patio in the garden, the right tiles can make the space look great and be practical to maintain. We use tiles suitable for outdoor use with proper adhesives and grout.

Large format porcelain tiles are popular for patios now. They give a contemporary look with minimal grout lines. Stone pavers and natural stone are other options depending on the look you want. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling can advise on what tiles suit your outdoor area.

Outdoor Adhesives and Grout

Outdoor tiling requires specific products. The adhesive needs to be flexible to cope with thermal movement because outdoor areas expand and contract with temperature changes. Standard indoor adhesive will fail outdoors. We use flexible adhesives designed for external use and epoxy or polymer modified grouts that handle the conditions.

Same goes for grout. Standard cement grout absorbs water and can crack or crumble outdoors. Epoxy grout is more expensive but it lasts longer in outdoor applications. Its particularly important around pools where the grout is constantly exposed to water and pool chemicals.

Drainage

Proper drainage is essential for outdoor tiling. Water needs somewhere to go. We create falls in the tiling toward drainage points so water runs off rather than pooling. For larger areas we might install strip drains or drainage channels. The falls typically need to be 1:100 minimum which means 10mm drop per metre.

For balconies over living areas the drainage is particularly important. We ensure water drains properly and doesnt sit against walls or penetrate the building.

Pool Retiling

We also retile existing pools including waterline tiles that often need replacing over time. Pool tiles cope with constant water exposure, chlorine and thermal stress so they do wear out eventually. Replacing waterline tiles can refresh the look of an older pool without a complete renovation.

Had a pool retiling job in Mosman where the waterline tiles had cracked and grout was falling out. We removed the old tiles, prepared the surface and installed new mosaic tiles around the waterline. The pool looks much better now and the owners got a few more years out of their existing pool before considering a bigger renovation.

Driveway and Entrance Tiling

Some outdoor tiling jobs include driveways and entrance areas. These need tiles rated for vehicle traffic if cars will drive on them. The substrate needs to be strong enough to handle the load. We assess each job to ensure the right approach.
