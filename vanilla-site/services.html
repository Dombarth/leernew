Tiling Services Sydney

Professional tiling and waterproofing solutions

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide comprehensive tiling services for residential and commercial properties throughout Sydney. From small repair jobs through to complete renovations, the team have the experience to handle any tiling project.

Wall Tiling Sydney

Wall tiling is one of our core services. We install all types of wall tiles including ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, glass and mosaic tiles. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have completed hundreds of wall tiling projects from kitchen splashbacks to feature walls in living areas.

For bathroom wall tiling we ensure proper waterproofing is done first. Thats critical for wet areas. We also pay attention to tile layout and cuts around fixtures so everything looks perfect. Each wall tiling job starts with proper surface preparation - if the walls arent flat and solid the tiles wont last.

We work in all areas of Sydney including the eastern suburbs, north shore and inner west regions. Most wall tiling jobs can be completed within two to three days depending on the size of the area.

Floor Tiling

Floor tiling requires different techniques than wall tiling. The substrate needs to be perfectly level and strong enough to support foot traffic. As such we spend alot of time on floor preparation including screeding and levelling where required.

We install all types of floor tiles from small mosaics to large format 600x600mm tiles and bigger. Natural stone floors like marble and travertine need special adhesives and sealers which we are experienced with. For outdoor areas we use non slip tiles and ensure proper drainage falls.

Sydney Premium Tiling are experienced in both residential and commercial floor tiling. We've done everything from small bathroom floors to large retail spaces. The team understand the importance of minimal disruption for businesses so we can work after hours if needed.

Bathroom Renovations

Complete bathroom renovations are a significant part of our work. We handle all the tiling and waterproofing aspects while coordinating with your plumber and electrician. If you dont have trades organised we can recommend reliable people we work with regularly.

Every bathroom renovation starts with proper waterproofing. We use quality membrane systems and provide certificates for all waterproofing work. The waterproofing needs to be done correctly - we see too many bathrooms with water damage from poor workmanship by other tilers.

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have renovated bathrooms in all types of properties from heritage terraces in Paddington to modern apartments in Chatswood. We understand different architectural styles and can advise on tile selection to suit your home.

Most standard bathroom renovations take between five to seven days for the tiling and waterproofing work. This doesnt include plumbing or electrical work which needs to be scheduled separately.

Bathroom Tiling Services

Bathroom tiling is our specialty. We handle shower recesses, bathroom floors, feature walls and vanity splashbacks. Each area has specific requirements - shower floors need proper falls to the drain, walls need waterproofing behind tiles, floors need non slip ratings.

For shower tiling we recommend using epoxy grout in the wet areas. Its more expensive than standard grout but it doesnt absorb water and resists mould growth. We also install shower niches and shelves properly waterproofed and integrated into the tiling.

The team are experienced with all bathroom tile types including porcelain, ceramic, natural stone and even timber-look tiles. We can create feature walls with mosaic tiles or keep it simple with large format tiles for a modern look.

Kitchen Tiling

Kitchen splashbacks are one of the most common tiling jobs we do. A well-tiled splashback protects your walls and adds style to the kitchen. We install subway tiles, mosaic patterns, large format tiles and natural stone splashbacks.

The tricky part of kitchen tiling is working around powerpoints and getting clean cuts around windows and cabinets. Our tilers are experienced in these detailed cuts and we always use trim pieces for a professional finish. We also seal the junction between tiles and benchtop properly to prevent water ingress.

Kitchen floor tiling needs to be durable and easy to clean. We recommend porcelain tiles for kitchen floors as their harder wearing than ceramic. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling can also install underfloor heating systems beneath your kitchen floor tiles if required.

Outdoor and Pool Tiling

Outdoor tiling has different requirements than indoor work. The tiles need to handle weather extremes, the adhesive must be flexible to cope with movement, and proper drainage is essential. We use specialised adhesives and grouts designed for external use.

For pool areas we install slip-resistant tiles and coping tiles around pool edges. Pool tiling requires specific adhesives that can handle constant water exposure and chlorine. We also retile existing pools including the waterline tiles that often need replacing.

Balcony tiling is another specialty area. Proper waterproofing is critical for balconies to prevent water damage to the structure below. Sydney Premium Tiling are licensed waterproofers and provide certificates for all balcony waterproofing work. We create proper falls to drainage points so water doesnt pool on your balcony.

We service all Sydney areas for outdoor tiling including the northern beaches, eastern suburbs and Hills District regions.

Other Services

We also provide tiling repairs, regrouting services, tile removal, stone polishing, sealing and waterproofing. If its something simple like replacing a cracked tile we can usually do it the same day. For insurance work we can provide quotes and deal directly with your insurer.

Commercial tiling is another area we specialise in. We work with builders, architects and project managers on commercial fitouts. The team understand the importance of meeting deadlines and working around other trades on commercial sites.
