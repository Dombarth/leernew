Bathroom Renovations Sydney

Complete bathroom renovation services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we handle the tiling and waterproofing aspects of bathroom renovations throughout Sydney. The team have renovated hundreds of bathrooms over the past eighteen years from small ensuites in apartments through to large family bathrooms in heritage homes. We work alongside your plumber and electrician to get the job done properly.

What We Do

A bathroom renovation involves multiple trades. We handle the demolition, waterproofing, tiling and finishing. Plumbing and electrical need to be done by licensed tradies in those areas. If you dont have a plumber or electrician lined up we can recommend people we work with regularly who we know do good work.

The tiling and waterproofing is often the biggest part of a bathroom reno both in terms of time and visual impact. Getting this right makes the difference between a bathroom that looks good for years and one that causes problems down the track.

Waterproofing First

Every bathroom renovation starts with waterproofing. This isnt optional. In NSW waterproofing is a licenced trade and needs to be done to Australian Standards. We are licenced waterproofers and provide certificates for all waterproofing work. You'll need this certificate if you ever sell the property.

We've seen alot of bathrooms over the years where the waterproofing was done poorly or not at all. Water gets behind tiles, into the substrate, and causes damage to the structure. Sometimes it leaks into the room below. Had a job in Balmain where we were called in to fix a bathroom that another tiler had done. The waterproofing membrane hadnt been applied properly around the shower hob and water was getting into the wall cavity. We had to strip everything out and start again. Its not a cheap fix when it goes wrong.

Demolition and Prep

Most bathroom renovations start with removing everything thats there. Old tiles, fixtures, sometimes the substrate needs to come out too if its damaged. We handle all the demolition and disposal. The walls and floors need to be properly prepared before waterproofing can happen.

In older homes we often find things during demolition that need to be addressed. Rotted framing from old water damage, asbestos in older fibro sheets, that sort of thing. Its better to find these issues and deal with them properly than cover them up and have problems later.

Tile Selection

We can advise on tile selection if you want but most people come to us with their tiles already chosen. Thats fine. If you're not sure what tiles to use we can discuss whats suitable for bathrooms. Floor tiles need to have appropriate slip ratings for wet areas. Some tiles are better in showers than others. Natural stone looks beautiful but needs sealing and more maintenance.

Had a customer in Double Bay who wanted marble on the shower floor. We talked them through the pros and cons and they decided to go with porcelain tiles that look like marble instead. Alot easier to maintain and still looks great.

Shower Installations

Showers are the most critical part of any bathroom tiling job. The waterproofing needs to be perfect, the falls need to be right so water drains properly, and the tiles need to be cut precisely around fixtures and niches. We install shower niches properly integrated into the waterproofing with proper falls so water drains out of the niche.

For shower floors we usually recommend epoxy grout. Its more expensive than standard grout but it doesnt absorb water and resists mould. In a shower recess thats constantly wet it makes a real difference to how the grout looks over time.

Bathroom Styles

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have renovated bathrooms in all types of properties. Heritage terraces in Paddington with period features that need to be respected. Modern apartments in Chatswood where everything is sleek and minimal. Family homes in the Hills District with large format tiles throughout. Each style has different requirements and we adapt our approach accordingly.

We dont push particular styles or tiles. We work with what you want and make sure its installed properly. Thats our job.

Timeline

A standard bathroom renovation takes about five to seven days for the tiling and waterproofing work. This doesnt include the plumbing and electrical work which happens before and after. The waterproofing membrane needs time to cure before tiling can start. Grout needs time to cure before the bathroom can be used.

Larger bathrooms or those with complex layouts take longer. If we need to repair structural issues or deal with asbestos removal that adds time. We'll give you an accurate timeline in our quote based on your specific bathroom.
