Tiling Services Sydney CBD

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the aspects of wall tiling, floor tiling, bathroom renovations and waterproofing for residential and commercial properties throughout the Sydney CBD. The team have been working in the city for over eighteen years now and we know the area well.

Alot of our work in the CBD involves apartment renovations and commercial fitouts. We do everything from small bathroom updates through to full floor tiling in office buildings and retail spaces. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling are experienced with the access requirements and strata rules that come with working in apartment buildings which is something that not all tilers in Sydney are familiar with.

We service customers throughout the Sydney CBD including Circular Quay, The Rocks, Barangaroo, Darling Harbour, Haymarket, Surry Hills, Ultimo, Pyrmont and Chippendale areas. For jobs in the city we can usually get there same day for a quote depending on what else is on.

Bathroom Tiling and Renovations

Bathroom tiling is probably the most common type of work we do in the CBD. Whether its a full renovation or just replacing some cracked tiles the team can handle it. We are fully licensed for waterproofing which is required for any wet area work in NSW. As such we are able to provide waterproofing certificates on completion.

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have completed hundreds of bathroom renovations in apartment buildings across the city. We understand the importance of getting the waterproofing right especially in multi storey buildings where a leak can cause problems for the apartments below.

Commercial Tiling

For commercial properties in the Sydney CBD we offer tiling services for offices, retail shops, restaurants and common areas. Floor tiling, feature walls, splashbacks for commercial kitchens, you name it. The team are experienced with working around business hours and can do after hours work if thats whats required.

We are members of the HIA and carry full public liability insurance and workers compensation. All work is carried out to Australian Standards and we provide a workmanship guarantee on every job.
