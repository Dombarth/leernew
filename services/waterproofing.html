Waterproofing Sydney

Licensed waterproofing for wet areas and balconies

At Sydney Premium Tiling we are licenced waterproofers providing waterproofing services for bathrooms, balconies, showers, laundries and other wet areas throughout Sydney. Waterproofing is a licenced trade in NSW and needs to be done to Australian Standards. We provide certificates for all waterproofing work which you'll need if you ever sell the property.

Why Waterproofing Matters

Waterproofing is the foundation of any wet area tiling job. If the waterproofing fails water gets behind the tiles and into the substrate. Over time this causes damage to the structure, mould growth, and eventually water leaking into other parts of the building. We've been called in to fix alot of bathrooms and balconies over the years where the waterproofing wasnt done properly in the first place.

Had a job come in from an apartment building in Bondi where water was leaking from a bathroom into the unit below. The bathroom had been renovated a few years earlier but the waterproofing hadnt been done correctly. The membrane wasnt properly bonded to the substrate and water was getting through. We had to strip everything out, redo the waterproofing properly, and retile the whole bathroom. Cost the owner alot more than doing it right the first time would have.

Bathroom Waterproofing

Every bathroom renovation requires waterproofing. In NSW you need a licensed waterproofer to do the work and issue a certificate. The waterproofing membrane goes on the floor and up the walls to a specified height. Shower recesses need full waterproofing to the ceiling height. The membrane needs to be properly bonded to the substrate and all joints, corners and penetrations need to be treated correctly.

We use quality membrane systems and follow the manufacturers specifications. The membrane needs time to cure before tiling can start. Rushing this step is a common mistake that leads to problems later. We allow proper curing time on every job.

Shower Waterproofing

Showers are the most critical area for waterproofing. Theyre constantly exposed to water and if the waterproofing fails the damage can be significant. We waterproof shower recesses from floor to ceiling including the hob, walls, and floor. All corners get reinforced with tape or bond breaker. The drain penetration is properly sealed. Every detail matters.

Shower niches need careful attention too. The waterproofing membrane needs to continue into the niche with proper corners and the niche floor needs to fall outward so water drains rather than pooling inside. We see alot of shower niches where this wasnt done correctly.

Balcony Waterproofing

Balcony waterproofing is critical work especially for balconies over living areas. If water penetrates through a balcony it damages the structure and the space below. Water damage to internal ceilings and walls from failed balcony waterproofing is expensive to repair.

The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have done alot of balcony waterproofing over the years. The membrane needs to be suitable for external use and handle thermal movement. The floor needs proper falls to drainage points. Upturns at walls and door thresholds need to be high enough and properly detailed. We provide certificates for all balcony waterproofing work.

Leaking Shower Repairs

A significant part of our waterproofing work is fixing leaking showers. Sometimes we can repair the waterproofing without removing all the tiles but often the best solution is to strip out and start again. We assess each situation and advise on the most practical approach.

Common causes of leaking showers include membrane failure, poor corner details, failed drain connections, and cracked tiles or grout that allow water through. Had a shower in Newtown where the grout had cracked and water was getting through to the timber floor framing. The framing was starting to rot. We stripped the shower, replaced the damaged framing, waterproofed properly and retiled. The owners caught it before the damage got worse.

Laundry and Wet Area Waterproofing

Laundries and other wet areas also require waterproofing under the Australian Standards. The requirements are similar to bathrooms with membrane on floors and up walls. We waterproof laundries as part of bathroom renovations or as standalone jobs.

Waterproofing Process

Proper waterproofing follows a process. The substrate needs to be clean, dry and properly prepared. We prime the surface then apply the membrane in multiple coats allowing curing time between coats. Corners and joins get reinforced. Penetrations like drains get properly sealed. Once the membrane is fully cured we can tile over it.

We use quality Australian made membrane systems that comply with Australian Standards. The products come with technical data sheets and we follow the manufacturers specifications for application.

Certificates

We provide waterproofing certificates for all work. In NSW waterproofing of wet areas is considered building work and requires a certificate from a licensed waterproofer. If you sell your property youll need to provide this certificate. If you're buying a property that had recent bathroom renovations ask for the waterproofing certificate. No certificate is a red flag.
