Northern Beaches Tiling Services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide tiling services throughout the Northern Beaches areas from Manly all the way up to Palm Beach. The team have been working on the beaches for over eighteen years and we do alot of work in the area. Bathrooms, outdoor areas, pool surrounds, you name it.

Living on the Northern Beaches means outdoor living is a big part of life. As such we do alot of outdoor tiling work including pool surrounds, alfresco areas, balconies and patios. The team understand the specific requirements for outdoor tiling including the right adhesives and non slip finishes that are needed.

Outdoor Tiling and Pool Surrounds

Pool surrounds are one of our specialties on the Northern Beaches. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling have tiled hundreds of pool areas over the years. We use the right adhesives for wet areas and make sure the falls are correct for drainage. Non slip tiles are a must around pools and we can advise on the best options.

For outdoor entertaining areas we work with all tile types including porcelain pavers, natural stone and travertine. The coastal environment on the Northern Beaches means tiles need to handle salt air and weather extremes. We know what works and what doesnt.

Bathroom Renovations

We also do plenty of bathroom renovations on the Northern Beaches. Whether its a full renovation in Freshwater or just retiling a shower in Avalon the team can help. We are licensed waterproofers and can provide certificates on completion which is required for any wet area work in NSW.

The coastal style bathrooms that are popular on the beaches often feature subway tiles, fish scale tiles, and natural stone. The team have done alot of these over the years and understand how to get the look right.
