Hills District Tiling Services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide professional tiling services throughout the Hills District areas. The team have been servicing Castle Hill, Baulkham Hills, Kellyville and surrounding suburbs for over eighteen years now. We do bathroom renovations, floor tiling, wall tiling, outdoor areas and waterproofing for both residential and commercial properties.

The Hills District has seen alot of development over the years with new homes going up everywhere. We work on both new builds and existing homes. For new builds we work alongside builders to complete the tiling to their schedules. For existing homes we handle everything from small repairs through to full bathroom renovations.

Bathroom Renovations Hills District

Bathroom renovations are a big part of what we do in the Hills District. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling handle the full job from demolition through to final tile installation. We are licensed waterproofers which is important for any wet area work. As such we can provide waterproofing certificates on completion.

Whether its a small ensuite update or a full main bathroom renovation we can help. We work with all tile types including large format tiles which are popular at the moment. Large format tiles require specific installation techniques to avoid lippage and the team have plenty of experiance with these.

Floor Tiling

Floor tiling throughout the home is another common job we do in the Hills area. The newer homes often have open plan living which means large areas of floor tiling. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling are experienced with this type of work and can handle large format porcelain tiles without problems.

For outdoor areas we do alfresco tiling, patio areas, pool surrounds and balconies. The bigger blocks in the Hills District often have outdoor entertaining areas that benefit from quality tiling.
