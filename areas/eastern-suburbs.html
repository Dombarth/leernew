Eastern Suburbs Tiling Services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in all aspects of tiling and waterproofing for residential properties throughout Sydneys Eastern Suburbs. The team have been working in this part of Sydney for over eighteen years and we have completed alot of work in the area from Bondi through to Randwick and everywhere in between.

We do bathroom renovations, floor tiling, wall tiling, kitchen splashbacks and outdoor tiling. The Eastern Suburbs have some beautiful older homes and the team are experienced with working on heritage properties where there are often specific requirements around what can and cant be done.

Bathroom Tiling Bondi and Surrounds

Bathroom work is probably our most common type of job in the Eastern Suburbs. Whether its a full renovation in Double Bay or just retiling a shower in Coogee we can help. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling are licensed waterproofers so we can handle the full job from demolition through to completion including the waterproofing membrane and certificates.

We work with all tile types including natural stone which is popular in the Eastern Suburbs areas. Marble, travertine, limestone, we have worked with it all. Natural stone does require specific installation methods and the team understand how to get the best results.

Suburbs We Cover

Sydney Premium Tiling service the entire Eastern Suburbs including Bondi, Bondi Junction, Bondi Beach, Tamarama, Bronte, Clovelly, Coogee, Randwick, Kensington, Kingsford, Paddington, Woollahra, Double Bay, Rose Bay, Bellevue Hill, Vaucluse, Watsons Bay, Point Piper, Darling Point and Elizabeth Bay. We also cover Centennial Park, Moore Park and the surrounding areas.

For jobs outside these areas just give us a call and we can let you know if we can help.

Quality and Insurance

Sydney Premium Tiling are fully licensed and insured. We carry public liability insurance and workers compensation and all work is completed to Australian Standards. The team are members of the HIA and we provide a workmanship guarantee on every job we do.

We dont cut corners. If the substrate isnt right we fix it before tiling. If the falls arent correct for drainage we sort that out too. Its the stuff you cant see that makes the difference in the long run.
