Local tiling services for the Inner West

Inner West Tiling

At Sydney Premium Tiling we specialise in the areas of bathroom renovations, floor tiling, wall tiling and waterproofing for homes throughout Sydneys Inner West. The team have been working in this part of Sydney for alot of years now and we know the area well. From the old workers cottages in Newtown through to the newer apartments in Rhodes we have done it all.

The Inner West has a real mix of property types which keeps things interesting. Terrace houses, federation homes, californian bungalows, art deco apartments, and new builds. Each type has its own challenges when it comes to tiling and the team at Sydney Premium Tiling have experience with all of them.

Bathroom Renovations

Bathroom renovations are our bread and butter in the Inner West. Alot of the older homes in areas like Balmain, Rozelle and Leichhardt have original bathrooms that are ready for an update. The team handle everything from the demolition through to the final tile installation. We are licensed waterproofers which is important for any wet area work.

For the older terrace houses we understand the challenges that come with limited access and working in tight spaces. The team are used to carrying materials through the house when there isnt rear access which is pretty common in the Inner West.

Floor and Wall Tiling

Beyond bathrooms we also do floor tiling throughout the home, kitchen splashbacks, laundry tiling and outdoor areas. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling work with all tile types from basic ceramics through to large format porcelain and natural stone.

For floor tiling in older homes we often need to level the substrate first. The floors in the old workers cottages and terraces are rarely level and getting this right is important for a quality finish. We dont just tile over whatever is there.

Areas We Service

Sydney Premium Tiling cover the entire Inner West including Newtown, Enmore, Marrickville, Petersham, Stanmore, Leichhardt, Lilyfield, Rozelle, Balmain, Birchgrove, Drummoyne, Five Dock, Burwood, Strathfield, Ashfield, Summer Hill, Dulwich Hill, Canterbury, Campsie and surrounding suburbs. We also service the inner city areas like Glebe, Annandale and Forest Lodge.
