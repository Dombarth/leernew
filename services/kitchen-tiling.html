Kitchen Tiling Sydney

Splashbacks, floors and feature walls

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide kitchen tiling services for homes and businesses across Sydney. Kitchen splashbacks are one of the most common jobs we do. A well-tiled splashback protects your walls from cooking splashes and adds style to the kitchen. The team have been tiling kitchens for over eighteen years and we know how to get the details right.

Kitchen Splashbacks

Splashback tiling seems straightforward but theres actually alot involved in getting it right. The tiles need to sit properly against the benchtop with the right gap. Cuts around powerpoints need to be precise. The layout needs to be planned so you dont end up with tiny slivers of tile at the edges or in corners. We see alot of splashbacks done by other tilers where the layout wasnt thought through properly.

Had a kitchen splashback job come in from a customer in Woollahra. They wanted subway tiles with a brick pattern layout. Simple enough but the tricky part was the window above the sink. We had to plan the tile layout so the pattern worked around the window without awkward cuts. Took some measuring but the result looked like it was meant to be there.

Tile Options for Splashbacks

Subway tiles are still the most popular choice for kitchen splashbacks. The classic white subway tile works with almost any kitchen style. We also do coloured subways, beveled edges and different sizes. Laying them in brick pattern is standard but we can do herringbone, vertical stack or other patterns if thats what you want.

Large format tiles give a more contemporary look with fewer grout lines. Glass tiles reflect light and add colour. Natural stone like marble creates a premium finish but needs sealing. Mosaic tiles work well for feature areas. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling can advise on whats suitable for your kitchen if your not sure.

Splashback Installation

Splashback tiling typically takes one to two days depending on the size and complexity. We remove any existing splashback material, prepare the wall surface, and install the new tiles. We use quality adhesives and grout designed for kitchen environments. The junction between tiles and benchtop gets sealed properly to prevent water getting in.

Working around powerpoints and light switches requires careful cutting. We always turn off power to those circuits before working near them. The tiles get cut precisely so the powerpoint covers sit flush against the tile surface.

Kitchen Floor Tiling

Kitchen floors need to be durable and easy to clean. They cop alot of traffic and inevitable spills. Porcelain tiles are our go-to recommendation for kitchen floors because theyre harder wearing than ceramic and more water resistant. Large format porcelain tiles are popular now for that seamless modern look.

We did a kitchen floor for a family in Castle Hill who had young kids. They wanted something that would handle the wear and tear. We installed 600x600mm porcelain tiles in a neutral colour that hides marks well. The substrate needed levelling first but once that was done the tiling went smoothly.

Underfloor Heating

Tiles and underfloor heating work really well together. The tiles conduct heat efficiently and hold warmth longer than timber. We can install kitchen floor tiles over underfloor heating systems using the right adhesives to cope with thermal expansion. If you're planning underfloor heating with new floor tiles we can discuss how it all works together.

Timber Look Kitchen Tiles

Timber look tiles give you the warmth of a timber floor without the maintenance concerns. In a kitchen where water spills happen this is a real advantage. The technology has improved alot and some of these tiles look very realistic. We lay them in a staggered pattern like real floorboards with random staggers so it looks natural.

Commercial Kitchen Tiling

We also tile commercial kitchens for restaurants and cafes. Commercial kitchens have specific requirements around hygiene and durability. Non slip floor tiles, easy to clean wall finishes, proper falls to floor drains. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling understand what commercial kitchens need and can work with your fitout schedule.
