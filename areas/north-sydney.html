North Sydney Tiling Services

At Sydney Premium Tiling we provide professional tiling services throughout the North Sydney and Lower North Shore areas. The team have been servicing this part of Sydney for over eighteen years and have completed hundreds of jobs in the area from Neutral Bay through to Chatswood.

We specialise in bathroom renovations, floor tiling, wall tiling and waterproofing for both residential and commercial properties. Alot of our work on the lower north shore involves apartment renovations and we are familiar with the strata requirements that come with this type of work.

Bathroom Renovations North Sydney

Bathroom renovations are one of the most common jobs we do in North Sydney. The team at Sydney Premium Tiling handle everything from the demolition through to the final tile installation. We are licensed waterproofers which means we can do the waterproofing membrane and provide certificates on completion.

Whether its a small ensuite update or a full main bathroom renovation we can help. We work with all types of tiles including porcelain, ceramic, natural stone and large format tiles. The team are experienced with the older style apartment buildings in North Sydney which often have their own challenges when it comes to waterproofing and substrate preparation.
